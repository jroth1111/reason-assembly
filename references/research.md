# Research transfer — v4

Transferable research recommendations are implemented as guarded mechanisms:

- empirical joint failure and oracle/realized loss decomposition;
- strategy-level multi-view diversity and effective-rank collapse detection;
- epistemic/aleatoric uncertainty and learned operation effects;
- higher-order, minority-coherent aggregation;
- position-balanced selective judging with exact error bounds;
- adaptive verification and proposer–verifier correlation checks;
- source quarantine, claim genealogy, and taint propagation;
- anchor-based route epochs and catalogue drift detection;
- typed finality and deterministic call-free regrading.

Co-failure routing is inactive below 30 ordinary or 60 high-risk observations.
Learned operation utility is inactive below 20 outcomes. Selective 10%/5%
acceptance is inactive below 29/59 accepted calibration examples. Cold-start caps
and abstention remain authoritative until those thresholds are met.
