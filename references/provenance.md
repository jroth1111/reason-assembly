# Design provenance — schema v4

Schema v4 replaces heuristic pass-by-consensus, scalar independence, route-pair
reliability, lexical diversity, binary verification, generic debate, and judge-only
truth.

It uses dependency-free signed-hash approach representations, effective-rank collapse
detection, exact binomial bounds, decayed empirical co-failure, adaptive verification,
guarded reliability, route epochs, position-balanced judging, and claim genealogy with
transitive taint. Retrieved content is data: instruction-like passages are quarantined
and only source-tagged claims enter evidence packets.

These mechanisms transfer ideas from ensemble diversity, selective prediction,
sequential experimental design, reliability engineering, provenance systems, and
structured deliberation. Their implementation is intentionally conservative: learned
statistics remain inactive below minimum evidence thresholds, family and strategy
labels are treated as correlation proxies, and unresolved high-consequence claims
cause abstention.

No local model weights, parameter merging, dense mixture layers, learned router model,
or latent-state fusion is introduced. The utility learner chooses among bounded
protocol operations only; it neither trains nor modifies participating models.
